# Misty Breez

## Notice

We're sunsetting Misty Breez and have unpublished it from the app stores.

The service it relies on is no longer available, so on-chain and Lightning payments cannot be made in the app.

Sending and receiving with a Liquid address still works, and is [how you can move your funds out](https://breez.technology/misty/).

We recommend downloading [Glow](https://breez.technology/glow), our new bitcoin app, powered by the Breez SDK.
