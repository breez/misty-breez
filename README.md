# Misty Breez

## Notice

We're sunsetting Misty Breez and have unpublished it from the app stores. The service it relies on is no longer available, so on-chain and Lightning payments cannot be made in the app. Sending and receiving with a Liquid address still works, and is how you move your funds out: see [How to Move Your Funds](https://breez.technology/misty/).

We recommend [Glow](https://breez.technology/glow), our new reference app, powered by the Breez SDK.
